# Retail Sales Analytics Business Report

## Executive Summary

This retail analytics project evaluates sales, profit, customer, product, discount, and regional performance. The analysis is designed to help leadership understand what is driving revenue, where profitability is under pressure, and which customer and product segments deserve additional focus.

The project combines Python analytics, MySQL analysis, and Power BI dashboard design into a production-quality business intelligence workflow. The recommended management focus is to grow high-margin categories, reduce unprofitable discounting, prioritize high-value customers, and monitor regional profitability more closely.

## Business Problems

1. **Revenue visibility is fragmented**  
   Leadership needs a single view of revenue performance by month, category, customer, and region.

2. **Profitability varies across products and regions**  
   Some products and markets may generate strong sales while contributing weak or negative profit.

3. **Discount strategy may be reducing margin**  
   High discount levels can increase sales volume but erode profitability if not controlled.

4. **Customer prioritization is unclear**  
   Sales and marketing teams need to identify VIP, high-value, growth, and low-value customers.

5. **Regional performance requires deeper monitoring**  
   State and region analysis is needed to guide local sales strategies and resource allocation.

## Key Insights

- **Revenue trend analysis** should be monitored monthly to identify growth, seasonality, and demand changes.
- **Profit margin is a more meaningful health indicator than revenue alone** because high sales can still be unprofitable.
- **Top customers often contribute a disproportionate share of sales**, making retention and account management critical.
- **Category and sub-category performance can reveal product lines that need pricing, promotion, or assortment changes.**
- **Discount band analysis helps identify whether promotions are profitable or margin-destructive.**
- **Regional and state-level reporting can highlight where growth opportunities or operational inefficiencies exist.**

## Recommendations

1. **Implement a monthly executive review cadence**  
   Review revenue, profit, order count, average discount, and profit margin every month.

2. **Protect margins through discount governance**  
   Set discount thresholds by category and require review for high-discount, low-margin transactions.

3. **Prioritize high-value customers**  
   Create account plans for VIP and high-value customers using customer segmentation outputs.

4. **Optimize product portfolio**  
   Expand profitable top-selling categories and investigate products with high revenue but low profit.

5. **Target regional improvement plans**  
   Compare state and regional profitability to determine where sales strategy, pricing, or operations need adjustment.

6. **Use Power BI for self-service analytics**  
   Deploy the four-dashboard report so stakeholders can filter by year, region, category, segment, and state.

## Future Improvements

- Add sales forecasting to estimate future revenue and inventory needs.
- Add customer cohort analysis to evaluate retention and repeat purchase behavior.
- Integrate acquisition cost or marketing spend to measure customer profitability more accurately.
- Add product return data if available to improve net revenue and profit analysis.
- Automate data refresh from the source system into MySQL and Power BI.
- Add row-level security in Power BI for regional managers.
